Who pays income tax

How much income tax people at different incomes are liable for, and how many of them there are.

Coverage. HMRC Table 2.5 in the current edition starts in 2023-24. Earlier years are in historic Table 2.5a editions that are not yet in this build, so they are gaps. Years after the latest Survey of Personal Incomes are HMRC projections and are marked Forecast. These are tax years (6 April to 5 April). Income bands are as HMRC publishes them; we do not re-band. Liabilities are not the same as cash receipts.
