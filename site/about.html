About

UK Public Money shows where public money comes from and where it goes, using only official published figures, and lets anyone check each one.

The rule

No number is published without a complete record of where it came from: the publisher, the file, its SHA-256 hash, the sheet, the cell, and every step we applied (such as converting £ million to £ billion). Every number links to a “Check this number” page with those details, so you can open the publisher’s own spreadsheet and find the same value.

What we do not do

  • We do not estimate, interpolate or fill missing years. Gaps are shown with a reason.
  • We do not combine sources inside a series, or silently align different definitions (UK vs GB vs England, cash vs accrued, tax year vs financial year).
  • We do not hide failed cross-checks. They are on the reconciliation page.
  • We do not track you. There are no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts.

Open data

Everything on this site is read from a static API you can use directly: api/v1/index.json (series list), api/v1/series/<id>.json, api/v1/provenance/<source>.json, reconciliation.json, definitions.json and Parquet files. Source data is under the Open Government Licence v3.0.
